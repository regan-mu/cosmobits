# CosmoBits SEO Audit — November 2026

Site: www.cosmobits.tech · Nov 25, 2026 · @Regan Muthomi

## Executive summary

**Verdict: strong foundation, but too small to rank.** The homepage is technically clean, yet the whole site is one page plus Privacy and Terms. Google has almost nothing to index, so CosmoBits can only rank for its own name, and it does not yet show up for that either.

**Biggest strength:** the homepage basics are done right. The title is 60 characters with the core keywords and location, there is one clear H1, a meta description, a canonical tag, Open Graph and Twitter cards, `en_KE` locale and index/follow robots. Many Nairobi competitors miss these.

**Top 3 priorities:**

1. **Give each service its own page.** Today AI, software, cloud, IT equipment and licensing are anchors (`/#cloud`) on one page. Anchors are not separate URLs to Google, so "cloud migration Kenya" has no page to rank.
2. **Fix brand visibility.** A search for "CosmoBits Technologies" and "cosmobits Nairobi" did not return the site. Add Organization/LocalBusiness schema, claim a Google Business Profile for APA Arcade, Hurlingham, and list on Clutch and GoodFirms, where most competitors already sit.
3. **Start publishing.** You already write advisories and features for social (PHP2Shell, Patch Week, AI Made in Africa). Publishing each as a blog post turns social effort into search traffic.

**On "top 1%":** in the Nairobi AI and software services space, competitors are mostly small studios with thin sites. A site with 5 service pages, 3 case studies, schema, directory listings and 2 posts a month would put CosmoBits ahead of nearly all of them within 6 to 9 months.

The biggest gap is format, not topic: competitors such as Afrisyntech already run a blog, a portfolio with case-study pages and an industries section. CosmoBits has none of the three. Build a pillar-and-cluster setup: each service page is a pillar, and blog posts link up to it.
